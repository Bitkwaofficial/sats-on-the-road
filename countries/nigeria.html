The country project

African ambition.
Local roots.

Nigeria is our first deep country focus: a place to connect education, everyday payments and the people who keep the movement going.

Our goal is to help people understand Bitcoin, practise self-custody and explore real merchant payments. We want local educators, businesses and community hosts to help shape what happens next.
