Sats On The Road / Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Sats On The Road is a grassroots Bitcoin campaign run by BitKwa. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the trip, answer you, and let you join the waitlist. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Who we are

Sats On The Road is operated by BitKwa. For any privacy question or request, email bitkwaofficial@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Waitlist sign-ups. When you use the Join page we collect the details you enter: role (merchant or user), name, and where relevant business name, category, country, city, phone and email. These are stored in a private Google Sheet we control.
  • Contact messages. When you use the contact form we receive your name, email and message so we can reply.
  • Wall of Support. If you sign the wall, the name, location and message you type are shown publicly on the page.
  • Basic analytics. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics that count page visits in aggregate. They do not track you across sites or build a profile, and no advertising cookies are set.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our analytics are cookieless. Your browser's local storage is used only for small conveniences, such as remembering that you dismissed the privacy notice, your language choice, and safely holding a waitlist entry if your connection drops so it can be sent when you are back online. This data stays on your device.

Third parties we rely on

To run the site we use a small number of trusted services, each with its own privacy policy: Vercel (hosting and analytics), Google (the Sheet and script behind the waitlist), Formspree (contact form delivery), Geyser (Bitcoin donations), Audiomack (the music player), and CoinGecko (the live price ticker). We never sell your data, and we only share what a service needs to do its job.

Data we collect on the road

When we teach and onboard people during the trip, we count events, not identities. We record how many people, merchants and ambassadors took part at each stop, never their names, phone numbers or wallet addresses. Verification is a count plus a receipt a person chooses to share, nothing stored. We film only with consent, blur faces on request, never film at borders or checkpoints, and strip location and device metadata from photos and video before publishing. The full rules are in our open field data policy.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and contact details for as long as the campaign is active and for a reasonable period afterward to follow up. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing bitkwaofficial@gmail.com. To remove a Wall of Support message, email us with the name and text and we will take it down.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date above.
